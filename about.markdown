Hello, internet.

My name is Furkan, which is Arabic for someone who tells right from wrong. Some will tell you the name suits me well, while others will swear I am always wrong. I try to be more “right” than wrong.

I come from Kashmir, a beautiful land with a turbulent history, and currently reside in the warm and friendly city of Mumbai.

Growing up, I thought I would end up as a physicist. Then a rock musician. Then a physicist again. Later, in my teens, I read about AI on Quora and finally ended up as a software engineer.

Other things I enjoy include reading books. I am currently reading Haroun and the Sea of Stories by Salman Rushdie. And music. I love music. It is magical. It transports me to a world of my own and brings me peace.

This year, I made two resolutions. The first is running. As I am writing this, it is January 16, 2026, and the AQI is very high, so I have not been able to run much. The second is this blog, where I will talk about my projects, thoughts, music, tech, and everything in between.

Whether the second resolution succeeds or not, time will tell.
