*NOTE: This post is purely the result of the human Furkan being prompted by the sight of his old pc desk.*

The clock was ticking 4:30 pm on May 23, 2026. I had just gotten home for a brief vacation from Mumbai. 

I unloaded and plugged in my phone for charging. I stared at my pc desk, the same desk that my dad had gotten me around 2008 when he got me my first computer. It brought back so many memories. A particular one lingered on. That of the scifi tv show "Person of Interest", which I had watched sitting at the same desk. 

The show revolves around "The Machine", an artificial intelligence agent which predicts crime before it occurs. What fascinates me is how closely the machine is like current LLMs in both how it was created and how it behaves. Harold Finch, one of the protagonists in the show created it based on the following principle:
*If you ingest enough data about everyone, patterns emerge before violent acts occur.*

Current LLMs are also being created following the same principle and they in essence also predict. They *predict* code if prompted with a coding problem, they *predict* poetry if asked to compose a poem, and so on. 

The show finished airing in 2016 and in just a couple of years LLMs came into existence. In the show, the machine is used as a mass surveillance tool by the government and LLMs also have the scope to be used similarly.

It's wild to think that a concept that I got introduced to from a show has materialized into reality while I am still using the same desk!
